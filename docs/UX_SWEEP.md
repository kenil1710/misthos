# UX and bug sweep (fresh eyes, before submission)

Date: 2026-10-05. A senior-designer / QA pass over every page and state, for every role, at 375 and 1440 px, in
light and dark.

**How:** a local production build (`next build` + `next start`) against the real Neon database (the live
program's paid Round 1 and open Round 2, about 17 QA programs with paid, approved, partial, escalated and rejected
work, a draft program), plus the live site for public pages. `apps/web/scripts/sweep.mts` drives it:

- **Coverage:** 45 page states × 2 widths × 2 themes = 180 loads.
- **Sessions:** minted locally for the real owner, a QA owner with many programs, a draft owner, the real
  contributor, a QA contributor and an anonymous visitor.
- **Per load:** screenshot, console errors/warnings, page errors (incl. hydration), failed requests,
  HTTP status, tab title, horizontal overflow, every date/time string, and axe (WCAG 2.2 AA + best practice) at
  1440 px in both themes.
- **Read-only:** nothing was submitted or signed.

**Result**

- **Before:** 168/180 loads clean, plus the visual review below.
- **After:** 176/180. The remaining 4 are the 404 page itself, where the browser logs its own 404 response, so this
  is expected. One transient `ERR_NETWORK_IO_SUSPENDED` on a rerun was the machine's network, not the app.
- **axe:** no violations left (was: `region` on docs, `heading-order` on Quickstart and the board, plus a latent
  `definition-list` on metrics that appeared once its list could be empty).
- **Overflow and console:** no horizontal overflow at 375 px, and no console errors or failed requests on any
  real page.

Screenshots: `docs/screenshots/ux-sweep/{before,after}/` (named below). Severity: **High** (wrong numbers or
misleading on a public page), **Medium** (confusing or inconsistent, layout clearly off), **Low** (polish).

## Checked and fine

- **Navigation:** every sidebar, breadcrumb, back link and card link resolved (no 4xx/5xx); Back closes drawers
  and dialogs first (e2e `navigation.spec.ts`).
- **States:** empty (no submissions, no rounds, nothing to pay), many items (17 programs, 10 submissions), draft
  vs published, scheduled vs open vs paid rounds, paid vs approved vs partial vs rejected vs in review, wallet
  connected vs not, signed in vs out, no access.
- **Contrast** in both themes (axe `color-contrast`), focus rings only for keyboard (e2e).
- **Consistency:** badges, amounts (`formatUsdc`, truncating), addresses (`HexValue`), steppers and page headers.
  Tables scroll inside their card on phones by design; the page itself never scrolls sideways.
