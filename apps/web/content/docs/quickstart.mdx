---
title: Quickstart for owners
description: From an empty wallet to your first paid round.
---

You need a wallet on Arc testnet with some test USDC. Arc uses USDC for gas, so the same balance pays for your
transactions. Get test USDC from the [Circle faucet](https://faucet.circle.com).

<Steps>
<Step>

## Sign in with your wallet

Open [the app](/app) and connect your wallet. You sign a message (no transaction) to prove it's yours.

</Step>
<Step>

## Create a program

Choose **New program** and fill in the four steps:

1. **Basics**: name, description, and the public link (`/join/your-program`).
2. **Rubric**: categories of work (for example *Threads* or *Pull requests*), the source types each accepts, and the
   criteria the agent scores from 0 to 10. A category's points are the average of its criteria.
3. **Budget**: the rate per point in USDC and how long a round lasts.
4. **Limits**: the caps the vault enforces. See [Guardrails](/docs/guardrails) for what each one does. Keep the
   wallet cooldown at 24 hours or more unless you have a reason not to.

![The program settings page with the vault and its limits](/screens/new-program.png)

</Step>
<Step>

## Deploy and fund the vault

On **Settings**, deploy the vault: one transaction from your wallet creates a vault owned by you, with the Misthos
agent as its only other role. Then deposit USDC (an approval and a deposit). The program goes live once the vault
holds funds.

![Vault settings with deploy, deposit and limits](/screens/settings.png)

</Step>
<Step>

## Share the join link

Contributors join at `/join/your-program`. They sign in with X and prove their payout wallet with a signature.

</Step>
<Step>

## Review what the agent sends you

Most submissions are decided automatically. The rest wait in **Submissions** with the agent's reasons, the flags it
raised and a recommended amount. Approve, change the amount, or reject with a reason the contributor will see.

![Reviewing an escalated submission](/screens/review-drawer.png)

</Step>
<Step>

## Pay the round

When the round ends (or when you choose **Close round now**), the agent re-checks every approved item, registers
payout wallets, and proposes the round to the vault. Rounds at or below your approval threshold are paid
automatically. Above it, you approve the round with one transaction and the agent pays it.

![A paid round with its transactions and decision records](/screens/round-detail.png)

</Step>
</Steps>

Every step is in the program's **Audit log**, and every payout is on the public audit page at `/p/your-program`.
