Launch a campaign and let AI pay your community for real work. You fund a USDC vault and set the rules. People create
threads, articles and code about your project and paste the links. An AI agent checks every submission, scores it
against your rules and explains its decision. At the end of each round your vault pays them in USDC on Arc,
automatically and never above the limits the vault enforces.

In the app a campaign is called a **program**, and the people who take part are **contributors**.

## What it does

- **Checks the work in code first.** Who posted it, whether it's inside the round, whether it was copied, merged,
  deleted or submitted before. Things code can prove never depend on a model.
- **Scores it with Claude.** Against the categories and criteria you write, through a strict output schema.
- **Decides with plain rules.** Hard problems reject, uncertain cases come to you, clear work under your auto-approve
  cap is approved. [Every rule and flag](/docs/how-the-agent-decides).
- **Pays from your vault.** Caps per payout, per round and per day, your approval above a threshold, and a cooldown on
  new payout wallets, all enforced on-chain. [Guardrails](/docs/guardrails).
- **Signs everything.** Each decision is a record hashed and signed by the agent's wallet, and each payout on Arc
  carries a hash of the records it pays. [Verify one yourself](/docs/audit-trail).
